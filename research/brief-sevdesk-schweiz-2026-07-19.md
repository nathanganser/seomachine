# Content Brief: sevdesk Schweiz

**Primary keyword:** sevdesk schweiz
**Swiss search volume:** 50/month (campaign tracker, 19.07.2026)
**Canonical owner:** `/alternative/sevdesk`
**Search intent:** Commercial investigation with a decisive eligibility question: can a Swiss business use sevdesk?
**Audience:** Swiss freelancers and Einzelfirmen comparing familiar DACH accounting tools.
**Conversion goal:** Help readers rule the product in or out on Swiss suitability, then try Magic Heidi if they need a Swiss-first solo workflow.

## Direct answer

sevdesk is not positioned for companies based in Switzerland. Its Swiss domain explicitly says it is designed for companies based in Germany or Austria. The page should answer this before discussing features or pricing.

## SERP and product findings

- The exact-query SERP is led by `sevdesk.ch`, which contains the Switzerland eligibility notice. This is the fact Magic Heidi must quote and link.
- sevdesk is a credible product for its intended markets: invoicing, AI receipt capture, online banking, mobile apps, and German/AT bookkeeping workflows.
- German price pages currently show a permanent free tier plus paid plans from EUR 12.90/month. Prices, promotions, and commitments change; they are context only, not the decision criterion for Swiss users.
- Swiss searchers need a checklist centred on Swiss MWST, CHF, QR-bills, ESTV output, Swiss banks, and record export.

## Differentiation and fairness

- Acknowledge sevdesk's automation, breadth, and DACH track record.
- Do not call the interface difficult or claim unsupported feature gaps.
- The decisive difference is verified market fit: sevdesk publicly limits its intended company seats to Germany/Austria; Magic Heidi is built for Swiss solo businesses.
- State Magic Heidi's honest limits: no payroll, no double-entry accounting, no inventory/POS, and bank import is CSV rather than direct e-banking.
