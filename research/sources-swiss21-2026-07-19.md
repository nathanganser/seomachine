## Review signals represented as paraphrases

- A 12.02.2026 reviewer praises the reliable invoicing, simple overview, year-end workflow and value for a small agency.
- A 22.05.2026 reviewer criticises manual steps and failed recognition in a specific SBB receipt workflow.
- Other recent reviews mention support and licence activation. These are individual experiences.

## Excluded or qualified claims

- The Trustpilot sample is very small and self-selected. The page names the date and sample size and does not use the rating as a product-wide verdict.
- Swiss21 publishes several app and package names. Claims are assigned to the relevant ecosystem component and not presented as features of one native desktop app.
- “80'000 companies” is vendor-reported social proof; it may be cited only as the vendor's current published figure.
