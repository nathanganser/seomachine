# Content Brief: lexoffice Schweiz / Lexware Office Schweiz

**Primary tracked keyword:** lexoffice schweiz
**Swiss search volume:** 10/month (campaign tracker, 19.07.2026)
**Canonical owner:** `/alternative/lexoffice`
**Current brand:** Lexware Office (formerly lexoffice)
**Search intent:** Eligibility and alternative evaluation for a German product known under its former brand.
**Audience:** Swiss freelancers who encountered lexoffice recommendations from German sources.
**Conversion goal:** Give an authoritative no/yes answer, explain the rebrand, and route Swiss solo users toward an appropriate local tool.

## Direct answer

Lexware Office, formerly lexoffice, is not suitable for businesses based in Switzerland. The official support says it is exclusively suitable for Germany and cannot use valid country-specific tax rates, currencies or online banking outside Germany.

## SERP and product findings

- Search demand still uses the legacy brand `lexoffice`; the page must use that keyword without pretending it is the current product name.
- The official rebrand page says existing contracts and performance remain unchanged and consolidates lexoffice under the Lexware Office brand.
- The current list prices are S EUR 7.90, M EUR 12.90, L EUR 21.90 and XL EUR 32.90 per month, excluding German VAT. Temporary three-month discounts should not be presented as regular pricing.
- Official support gives an unusually definitive Swiss limitation. This is stronger evidence than feature-by-feature inference.

## Differentiation and fairness

- Acknowledge Lexware Office's established German ecosystem, tier clarity, German accounting and optional payroll.
- Do not imply it is a poor product. It is simply the wrong jurisdiction.
- Magic Heidi is appropriate for Swiss freelancers needing CHF, Swiss MWST and local QR invoices, but not for payroll, double-entry books or an ERP.

## Required page structure

1. Hero that pairs the legacy query with the current brand.
2. Direct official eligibility answer and rebrand note.
3. Swiss requirement checklist.
4. Balanced comparison with current regular prices.
5. Who should choose Lexware Office and who should choose Magic Heidi.
6. Migration/archive checklist.
7. Four to six FAQs targeting both brand names.
8. Source/verification block and trial CTA.

## SEO requirements

- Use `lexoffice Schweiz` in title, hero, opening and FAQs.
- Use `Lexware Office Schweiz` alongside it so the page remains current.
- Related terms: `lexoffice Alternative Schweiz`, `Buchhaltungssoftware Schweiz`, `CHF`, `Schweizer MWST`, `QR-Rechnung`.
- Link to `/vat-management`, `/invoicing`, `/pricing`, `/security`, and `/accounting`.

## Acceptance criteria

- Current and former brand names are explained in the first screenful.
- The Germany-only support statement is quoted only by paraphrase and linked to the official article.
- No German feature is presented as Swiss compliance.
- Temporary discounts are not used for a long-term price comparison.
