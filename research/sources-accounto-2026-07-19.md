## Excluded or qualified claims

- Old public PDFs and price comparison tables show historical Accounto prices, but the current official product pages do not confirm them. The page therefore says “Preis auf Anfrage”.
- No broad current independent Accounto review profile was found. Vendor success stories are not presented as independent reviews.
- Accounto's claim to be Switzerland's only fully automated accounting software is a vendor claim and is not repeated as an objective fact.
