## Search answer and positioning

Swiss21 is a broad ERP ecosystem, not just one invoice app. The permanent Starter plan is a real strength: CHF 0 with 1'000 documents per year, one user and one payroll. Basic costs CHF 21/month and unlocks advanced accounting and unlimited document recognition; Pro costs CHF 49/month. Magic Heidi is the narrower native-app option for solo freelancers and does not compete on payroll, CRM, POS, shop or full double-entry accounting.
