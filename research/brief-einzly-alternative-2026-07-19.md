**Secondary:** `einzly erfahrungen` (tracked, volume 0)

**Audience:** Swiss solo freelancers and sole proprietors below CHF 500'000 turnover.

## Search answer and positioning

einzly is a credible close competitor, not a straw man. It offers simplified accounting, QR invoices, both VAT methods, AI receipt scanning and camt.053 reconciliation at CHF 80/year for Basis or CHF 144/year for Pro. It is the price winner for users who prefer a browser/PWA workflow. Magic Heidi costs more but offers a permanent feature-complete free tier and native iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows apps.

## Claim guardrails

- Credit einzly for AI receipt scanning, both VAT methods, bank matching, quotes/orders and very low pricing.
- Do not claim a native app: einzly describes itself as a PWA added to the home screen.
- Explain that Einzly's Trustpilot sample is only 19 reviews and self-selected.
- State that neither tool fits payroll or full double-entry requirements.
- Use the current official annual and monthly prices, not older SERP snippets.
