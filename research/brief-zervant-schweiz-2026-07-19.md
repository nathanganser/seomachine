# Content Brief: Zervant Schweiz

**Primary tracked keyword:** zervant
**Secondary intent:** zervant schweiz, zervant alternative schweiz
**Swiss search volume:** 10/month for `zervant` (campaign tracker, 19.07.2026)
**Canonical owner:** `/alternative/zervant`
**Search intent:** Brand evaluation, pricing and Swiss suitability.
**Audience:** Swiss freelancers seeking a simple or free invoicing tool.
**Conversion goal:** Respect Zervant's invoicing strengths while explaining when a Swiss freelancer needs deeper local MWST, banking and expense workflows.

## Direct answer

Zervant is available with CHF pricing and has advertised Swiss QR invoices. It remains primarily an invoicing tool, not a complete Swiss bookkeeping and MWST solution. Its current price FAQ says payment reconciliation is not available in Switzerland.

## SERP and product findings

- Official pages emphasise invoices, offers, status tracking, reminders, recurring invoices, mobile use and time tracking.
- The German price page exposes CHF and identifies Switzerland as a price region.
- An official article updated 31.01.2024 says Zervant can create Swiss QR invoices. Because this claim is not prominent in the current feature list, readers should verify it inside the current product before migration.
- Public Zervant pages conflict: the German pricing table says unlimited free invoices, while its FAQ says five per month. The German and English pages also show different CHF tariff names/prices. Do not resolve the contradiction by guessing.
- The price FAQ explicitly lists countries where payment matching is available and omits Switzerland.

## Differentiation and fairness

- Acknowledge that Zervant is easy to start, offers CHF pricing and can be attractive for invoice-only use.
- Avoid calling it non-Swiss. It has more Swiss accommodation than sevdesk or Lexware Office.
- Position Magic Heidi on integrated Swiss MWST, AI receipt capture, Swiss bank CSV matching and freelancer accounting, not on generic “simplicity”.
- State Magic Heidi's honest limits: Zervant has time tracking and potentially a cheaper entry price; Magic Heidi has no payroll or full double-entry.

## Required page structure

1. Direct answer with verification date.
2. “What Zervant is good at” section.
3. Swiss checklist: QR-bill, CHF, MWST, bank matching, expenses, data location.
4. Comparison table with uncertainty labels where public sources conflict.
5. Best-fit recommendations for each product.
6. Switching checklist.
7. Four to six FAQs.
8. Source block and trial CTA.

## SEO requirements

- Use `Zervant` in title, hero and first answer; use `Zervant Schweiz` in at least two prominent components.
- Related terms: `Zervant Alternative`, `Rechnungsprogramm Schweiz`, `QR-Rechnung`, `CHF`, `MWST`, `Zahlungsabgleich`.
- Link to `/invoicing`, `/vat-management`, `/bank-statement-import`, `/ai-expenses-scanning`, and `/pricing`.

## Acceptance criteria

- Price/free-limit conflicts are disclosed instead of hidden.
- Swiss bank reconciliation is not claimed for Zervant.
- QR invoice support is dated to the official 2024-updated article and presented with a verification caveat.
- The recommendation distinguishes invoice-only from integrated Swiss admin needs.
