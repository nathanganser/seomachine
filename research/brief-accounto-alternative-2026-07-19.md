**Canonical owner:** `/alternative/accounto`

**Intent:** Navigational plus commercial evaluation. Searchers want the vendor, product scope, pricing and fit before speaking to a fiduciary.
**Audience:** Swiss sole proprietors and small service businesses deciding between a fiduciary-led full accounting setup and self-service freelancer software.

## Search answer and positioning

Answer the brand query directly: Accounto is a Swiss automated accounting platform aimed primarily at fiduciaries and the SMEs they manage. It is strong when a business needs full accounting, payroll, closing and shared live data. Magic Heidi is the narrower self-service option for solo freelancers who need invoicing, expenses and VAT, but no payroll or double-entry accounts.

Pricing must be described as **on request** because the current official pages do not publish a fixed package. Tell readers to request the combined software, onboarding, support and fiduciary cost in writing. Do not repeat old third-party prices as current facts.
