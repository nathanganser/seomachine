### Factual errors found and fixed in the kept posts
The keeper pass was meant to be a formatting and tone job. It surfaced substantive errors in the auto-generated text, all corrected:

- **`Art. 26 MWSTV` → `Art. 26 MWSTG`** in **42 files across all locales**. The mandatory-invoice-content article is Art. 26 MWSTG; the posts cited the ordinance instead of the act. This was the single most widespread error.
- **Verjährung**: the 10-year rule was attributed to Art. 130 OR (correct: **Art. 127 OR**), and the text implied a Mahnung interrupts prescription. It does not.
- **Zahlungsfristen**: asserted a statutory 30-day payment term with automatic default under Art. 102 OR. Swiss law has no general statutory payment period; corrected to Art. 75 / 102 I / 102 II OR with "30 Tage netto" as custom. Invoice requirements had been cited as "OR Art. 469" (that article covers Anweisung).
- **Verrechnungssteuer**: the article framed **Formular 25** as the route to reclaim *foreign* withholding tax. Formular 25 is the refund form for Swiss legal entities; foreign withholding tax goes via **DA-1**.
- **E-Rechnung**: claimed Magic Heidi offers ZUGFeRD export and eBill integration. It offers neither. Also two structural bugs — a FAQ keyed `questions:` where the component needs `items:` (it rendered empty) and an unclosed hero block.
- **QR-Rechnung switchover dated to October 2020** in four places (correct: October 2022); a claim that a QR-Rechnung requires a QR-IBAN (a normal IBAN works); a claim that an Einzelfirma name must carry the suffix "Einzelfirma" (it must carry the owner's family name); "Treuhänder" described as a protected professional title (it is not; only the qualifications behind it are); "SBB tickets are VAT-exempt"; the reduced accommodation rate given as 3.7% (correct: 3.8%); AHV self-employed rates stated as 10.1%/10.3% (softened to "rund 10%", the scale is degressive); and a worked VAT split that did not add up.
- **Stale competitor pricing**: "bexio CHF 52/Monat" appeared in at least eight posts. bexio's plans are CHF 35/42/69/119. All numeric claims removed in favour of a link to `/bexio-preise`.
- **Invented evidence**: named personas with precise outcomes (payment time 34 → 11 days, CHF 920 lost, CHF 2'025 input tax, "42% cheaper than bexio", hourly-rate tables presented as market data) read as fabricated testimonials and statistics. Reframed as anonymous examples or removed.
