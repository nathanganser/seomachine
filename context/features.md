# Magic Heidi Features & Benefits

This document outlines Magic Heidi's key features, benefits, and differentiators to inform content creation that drives trial conversions and customer acquisition.

## Core Value Propositions

### 1. **Comprehensive Invoicing Software**
- **Feature**: Everything a swiss freelancer needs. Offers, Invoices, Payment Reminders. Invoice sending per email. Professional design with client branding by default.
- **Benefit**: Command control center over a freelancer's business, which invoices are due, when was the offer sent, ... on desktop and phone. Create complex invoices with many products, services, add T&Cs, ...
- **Conversion Angle**: "Invoicing software for all your needs as a swiss freelancer."

### 2. **AI Powered Expense Scanning**
- **Feature**: Upload your file or take a picture, our AI will analyse and extract all relevant data.
- **Benefit**: Save time and reduce manual data entry by automatically extracting expense information.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Scan your expenses with AI and save hours of manual data entry."

### 3. **Swiss VAT Management**
- **Feature**: All Swiss VAT rates built in. Supports Effective Method and Net Tax Rate Method. VAT breakdowns per invoice, input VAT tracking on expenses, and tax-ready exports.
- **Benefit**: Never second-guess VAT calculations again. Freelancers can see what they owe, prepare MWST filings faster, and export clean documents for their Treuhänder.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Swiss VAT done right. Automatic calculations, both filing methods, and tax-ready exports."

### 4. **Bank Statement Import & Auto-Reconciliation**
- **Feature**: Import CSV statements from Swiss banks and automatically match payments to invoices using amount, date, reference number, client name, and historical patterns.
- **Benefit**: Know instantly which invoices are paid and which still need follow-up. No more manual cross-checking against bank statements.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Import your bank statements and let Magic Heidi match payments automatically."

### 5. **Native Apps on Every Platform**
- **Feature**: Native apps for iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows, plus a web app. Real-time sync across devices and one login for everything.
- **Benefit**: Invoice from your phone after a client meeting, scan expenses on the go, and check your business from any device.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Invoice anywhere. Scan expenses anywhere. One account, five platforms, always in sync."

### 6. **Client Management & Business Analytics**
- **Feature**: Unlimited clients, private notes, invoice history, revenue-per-client tracking, monthly revenue and expense charts, profit overview, and tax estimates.
- **Benefit**: Freelancers can see how their business is performing at a glance — who pays on time, where revenue comes from, and how much tax they may owe.
- **Conversion Angle**: "See exactly how your freelance business is performing in one simple dashboard."

### 7. **Free Tier With No Credit Card**
- **Feature**: Full-featured free plan with up to 3 invoices. No credit card required and no time limit. Pro unlocks unlimited invoices, quotes, expenses, and clients.
- **Benefit**: Start with real invoices before paying. No risk, no pressure, no hidden catch.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Start free. No credit card. No time limit. Upgrade only when you're ready."

## Technical Features

### Invoicing
- **Swiss QR-bill v2.3**: SIX-compliant QR invoices generated automatically.
- **Multi-language invoices**: Create invoices in DE, FR, IT, and EN.
- **Quotes and offers**: Create professional offers and convert them into invoices.
- **Payment reminders**: Follow up on overdue invoices.
- **Recurring invoices**: Automate monthly or yearly billing.
- **Partial payments and progress billing**: Support split payments and milestone billing.
- **Credit notes and refunds**: Handle corrections professionally.
- **Custom branding**: Add logo, colors, notes, T&Cs, and client-facing details.
- **Multi-currency**: Support 50+ currencies with automatic SNB exchange rates.
- **Invoice duplication**: Copy repeat invoices in one click.

### Expenses & Receipts
- **AI receipt scanning**: Extract date, amount, vendor, currency, and VAT automatically.
- **Photo upload**: Snap receipts from a phone and save them instantly.
- **PDF upload**: Upload supplier invoices and receipt documents.
- **Email import**: Forward receipts to a Magic Heidi email address.
- **Foreign currency expenses**: Convert expenses with SNB daily exchange rates.
- **Recurring expenses**: Track monthly subscriptions, rent, and regular costs.
- **Depreciating items**: Track assets over time.
- **Payment method tracking**: Attribute expenses to the correct payment method.
- **Multiple attachments**: Store several files on one expense entry.

### VAT & Tax
- **Swiss VAT rates**: Built-in support for 8.1%, 2.6%, and 3.8%.
- **Effective Method**: Track input VAT and output VAT.
- **Net Tax Rate Method**: Support Saldosteuersatz calculations.
- **VAT breakdowns**: Show VAT per invoice and per period.
- **VAT period totals**: Prepare quarterly or semi-annual summaries.
- **VAT rounding rules**: Built-in Swiss VAT rounding logic.
- **Estimated tax calculator**: See what you may owe before filing.
- **Tax-ready annual document**: Export documents for the Steuererklärung.

### Banking & Payments
- **Swiss bank CSV import**: Import statements from UBS, PostFinance, ZKB, Raiffeisen, Migros Bank, Neon, Yuh, and cantonal banks.
- **Auto-reconciliation**: Match payments to invoices automatically.
- **Multi-variable matching**: Match by amount, date, reference number, client name, and patterns.
- **Multiple bank accounts**: Manage several Swiss or foreign payment details.
- **Payment confirmations**: Send receipts when payments arrive.
- **Payment proof upload**: Attach evidence to any invoice.

- **iPhone app**: Invoice and scan expenses on the go.
- **Android app**: Manage freelance admin from Android devices.
- **Mac app**: Native desktop workflow for Mac users.
- **Windows app**: Native desktop workflow for Windows users.

## Common Questions & Objections

### "Is Magic Heidi only for freelancers or can SMEs use it too?"
**Answer**: Magic Heidi is built especially for Swiss freelancers and Einzelfirmen. If you are a solo business owner, it gives you the invoicing, expenses, VAT, and payment tracking you need without the complexity of SME tools. If you have employees and need payroll, Magic Heidi is not the right fit.

### "Can I really use it for free? What's the catch?"
**Answer**: Yes. The free plan lets you create up to 3 invoices and try the main features, including QR-bills, AI scanning, bank import, and VAT tracking. No credit card is required and there is no time limit.

### "Is my data safe? Where is it stored?"
**Answer**: Magic Heidi hosts data in Zürich, Switzerland. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit, automatic backups protect your files, and you can export a full ZIP archive of your data anytime.

### "Does Magic Heidi handle Swiss QR-bills properly?"
**Answer**: Yes. Magic Heidi supports SIX-compliant Swiss QR-bill v2.3 and automatically generates QR invoices so Swiss clients can pay with one scan.

### "How does the AI expense scanning work?"
**Answer**: Upload a photo, PDF, or forwarded email receipt. Magic Heidi analyses the document and extracts the date, amount, vendor, currency, and VAT information. You review and confirm before saving.

### "What if I do not like it?"
**Answer**: Pro plans include a 30-day money-back guarantee. You can export your invoices, expenses, receipts, PDFs, CSVs, and files anytime, so you are never locked in.
