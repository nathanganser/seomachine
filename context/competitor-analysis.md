## Primary Competitors

### Competitor 1: Bexio

**Company Overview**:

* **Website**: bexio.com
* **Primary Offering**: SME-focused invoicing, accounting, payroll, warehouse and business administration software
* **Target Audience**: Swiss SMEs, teams, businesses with employees, fiduciaries
* **Pricing**: CHF 45–75/month; no permanent free tier, 30-day trial only
* **Market Position**: Market leader

**Content Strategy**:

* **Blog URL**: No meaningful educational blog; primarily product pages, academy, webinars, courses and help docs
* **Publishing Frequency**: Very low for educational SEO content
* **Content Types**: Product pages, help documentation, academy content, webinars, courses
* **Average Article Length**: Thin or product-focused; limited true educational content
* **Content Quality**: Strong product documentation and brand authority, but weak long-form educational content for freelancers

**Top Performing Content**:

1. Product/category pages for Swiss accounting and invoicing software

   * Keyword: Rechnungssoftware Schweiz / Buchhaltungssoftware Schweiz
   * Estimated Traffic: High relative to category
   * Why It Works: Strong domain authority, long market presence, strong brand recognition and relevant product-page targeting

2. MWST-related product and support pages

   * Keyword: MWST Abrechnung Software
   * Estimated Traffic: Medium to high
   * Why It Works: Bexio benefits from category authority and broad SME positioning

3. Fiduciary and marketplace pages

   * Keyword: fiduciary/accounting software-related terms
   * Estimated Traffic: Medium
   * Why It Works: Marketplace ecosystem and directory-style content attract links and authority

**SEO Strengths**:

* Strong domain authority, estimated DR 65–75
* 10+ years of domain and brand history
* Large customer base of 100,000+ companies
* Marketplace backlinks and fiduciary directory authority
* Strong rankings for core category terms such as “Rechnungssoftware Schweiz” and “Buchhaltungssoftware Schweiz”

**SEO Weaknesses**:

* Almost no freelancer-specific educational content
* No meaningful AI scanning content
* No strong how-to guides or comparison content
* No permanent free tier to use as a content-led acquisition hook
* Content is mostly product-led, not problem-led

**Content Gaps**:

* Bexio alternative content
* “Bexio zu teuer” / price-sensitive comparison content
* Freelancer-specific invoicing and accounting guides
* AI expense scanning guides
* “Software für Selbständige ohne Mitarbeiter”

**Differentiation Opportunities**:

* Own the freelancer narrative Bexio ignores
* Create comparison content around price, complexity and setup time
* Position Magic Heidi as the lightweight, freelancer-first alternative
* Target “bexio alternative,” “bexio zu teuer,” and “bexio für selbständige” style searches

## Competitive Content Patterns

### Common Topics All Competitors Cover:

1. Product-led invoicing and accounting software pages

   * Standard approach: Describe features, pricing and target businesses
   * Magic Heidi differentiation: Lead with freelancer-specific workflows, setup speed, AI expense scanning and native apps

2. Help documentation and support content

   * Standard approach: Explain how to use the product
   * Magic Heidi differentiation: Turn support topics into public educational guides that answer pre-purchase questions

3. Pricing and plan pages

   * Standard approach: Feature tables and plan comparisons
   * Magic Heidi differentiation: Use pricing content to explain total cost, freelancer value and why CHF 25/month unlimited is simpler than SME pricing

4. Swiss compliance topics

   * Standard approach: Product-specific or documentation-heavy
   * Magic Heidi differentiation: Publish plain-language guides for non-accountants, especially around QR invoices, VAT/MWST and expenses

### Emerging Topics Some Competitors Cover:

* QR-Rechnung: Covered by Smallinvoice and QR invoice tools
