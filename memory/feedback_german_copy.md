Do not use **"ehrlich" / "ehrlicher"** in German titles, headlines, or CTAs.

**Why:** Nathan explicitly rejected "Ehrlicher Vergleich" as a title differentiator for the Buchhaltungssoftware article (2026-05-06). The word sounds off-brand — too self-congratulatory or performative in German.

**How to apply:** When the translate-components skill, the write command, or any agent generates German copy and reaches for "ehrlicher Vergleich", "ehrliche Bewertung", or similar constructions, replace with a direct descriptive alternative instead. Examples:
- "Ehrlicher Vergleich" → "6 Tools im Vergleich" or "Unabhängiger Vergleich"
- "Ehrliche Bewertung" → "Detaillierte Bewertung" or just the factual claim
